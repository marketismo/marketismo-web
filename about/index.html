How it started

I created Marketismo in January 2011. It was a time when social media and ecommerce were exploding and Marketing was starting to orbit around them, so I thought it was a good opportunity to start a blog and explain how Marketing, and the whole world, was changing. Back then I was doing my Master of International Business at Macquarie University in Sydney, surrounded by classmates from every continent and teachers who were opening our eyes to this new era. That was more than enough inspiration to start writing and enjoy the journey.

More than a decade later, AI is here, pushing not only Marketing but every corner of the organisation towards new boundaries, and I'm here to play with it.

I was born in Spain and I live in Madrid, but I've always had a preference for international experiences (I studied in the US and Australia), and I've worked in multinational companies across FMCG, tourism, automotive and education.

An intrapreneur at heart

You might think my work was very 'corporate', but the truth is that I've always worked as an 'intrapreneur', almost like in a start-up. At Coca-Cola, as an Assistant Brand Manager, it was my line manager and me trying new products and doing market research in the mixers category for cocktails, right when we were relaunching the tonic water Nordic Mist (now Royal Bliss). At Transhotel, I created a segmented display and SEM advertising system on the company's own booking platform, which brought in €500K from hotel chains.

Then, at Euromaster (Michelin Group), I was commissioned to launch and manage the ecommerce platform in Spain, with the P&L on my side of the desk. That meant creating an e-shop in an old-fashioned industry like vehicle repair shops. It was such an awesome experience: training the shops to manage orders, working with Marketing, Operations and Procurement to add new services in a way that people could choose them without being experts in car maintenance, partnering with brands like Apple, Pirelli and Shell, and working with the European ecommerce squad to improve conversion. The result: online sales up 15% year on year and the highest average ticket across Euromaster Europe.

The British Council

Then, in 2014, the British Council called me. I loved the project: creating the Marketing Department for the English teaching centres in Spain, as part of the Senior Management Team. The British Council is the UK's cultural organisation and a charity, so the mix of not-for-profit activities in arts and culture, plus the courses and exams we had to grow to raise funds, was pretty attractive. Spain was the British Council's biggest market, so the responsibility was huge. With a team of 5 we grew SQLs by 12% a year, launched new courses that added 10% in revenue, and won the OCARE 2020 award for the best B2C CSR campaign in Spain with #AprendoconBritishenCasa. Along the way I was also a visiting lecturer at IE Business School, presenting real British Council cases to Master in Market Research students.

In 2022 I moved to the global team as Global Senior Marketing Manager B2B, for English for Corporates. The mission was similar to the one in Spain, but in B2B and at a global scale: building the B2B Marketing department, its brand identity and campaigns in 20 countries that generated SQLs for the sales team, implementing HubSpot and connecting it with Salesforce, and leading a team of 9 plus the regional Marketing teams. Revenue grew 20% a year and doubled in 4 years.

Marketing with AI, hands on

I don't just talk about AI, I build with it, using tools like n8n and Claude Code, and I'm currently taking the Advanced Program in Business AI at ESIC. Each experiment on Marketismo is a working thing, made without programmers or agencies, to see what AI can really do for an organisation (and yes, this very page is built for GEO, so AI search engines can find it):

  • Calendar + AI: a concept portal for the British Council, with every course, exam and event in one calendar, an AI level test with a teacher in the loop, and demand-based pricing.
  • ALMA: a school platform whose AI agents turn grades, attendance and wellbeing data into personalised insight for directors, teachers, parents and students.
  • My Second Brain: my professional memory organised by AI, an interactive map of my Marketing career, skills and blog.
  • Guess the year: an app that names the song playing at the bar, plus a game for friends.

What's next

Now it's time to explore what AI can do for organisations, especially in the form of agents that automate processes so humans can spend more time thinking and creating. I'll combine it with my passion for cars, watches and music, looking for inspiration in those hobbies and experimenting with applying AI to them.

It's amazing how anyone with a bit of curiosity can create digital assets, apps and automations today. The only limits are your imagination and your token counter!
